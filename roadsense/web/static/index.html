    HOW IT WORKS

    From a bump on the road to a ticket the city can act on.

    1. 01

      Capture

      A YOLO model finds potholes and cracks in dashcam video and estimates size and severity.

    2. 02

      Redact

      Everything outside the damage is blurred on the device, and the server checks it again.

    3. 03

      Triage

      Agents verify the sighting, merge duplicates and pick the authority from map evidence.

    4. 04

      Approve & file

      A reviewer sees the exact payload. Only then is it sent, and follow-up tracks the repair.
