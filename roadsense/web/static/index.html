    From a bump on the road to a repair that holds.

    1. 01

      Capture

      A YOLO model finds potholes and cracks in dashcam video and estimates size and severity.

    2. 02

      Redact

      Everything outside the damage is blurred on the device, and the server checks it again.

    3. 03

      Triage

      Agents verify the sighting, merge duplicates and note who owns the road from map evidence.

    4. 04

      Review & repair

      A reviewer approves, an inspector sets a repair date, the crew marks it done, and later trips confirm the fix or reopen it.

    The city sees the pothole. Nobody else.
